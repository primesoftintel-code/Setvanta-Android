Setvanta privacy policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026. This policy also appears inside the Setvanta app.

Who provides Setvanta

Setvanta is published by PRIMESOFT INTERNATIONAL. It is an independently branded modification of the open-source openGym application; the openGym author and website are not Setvanta's publisher. For privacy questions, contact primesoftintel@gmail.com. The publisher's website is https://primesoftintel.netlify.app/.

Information handled by the app

Setvanta stores the information you enter or generate while training: routines, scheduled sessions, exercise choices, sets, repetitions, weights, rest settings, workout history, body-weight entries and goals, notes, and optional progress or custom-exercise photos and videos. If you add a gym card or scan its code, that value can also be saved. Local mode keeps this information on the device in WebView storage with an app-private state-file mirror. Custom media use app-private files. The app uses device permissions only for features you choose, including notifications, camera scanning, Health Connect and media selection.

Optional server connection and AI Coach

If you pair the app with a compatible server you control, Setvanta sends your profile and training state, and applicable media, to that server and synchronizes changes. Pairing uses a code and a bearer token. The Setvanta Play build requires HTTPS for the connection. The server operator's own retention, security and privacy practices also apply; Setvanta does not operate that server by default.

If you choose an AI Coach mode, the app may send selected plan, training, body-weight, profile and preference information to your configured model provider, directly with a key you provide or through your paired server. Coach use requires its separate in-app setup and consent. The provider or server operator may process and retain requests under their own terms. Do not enter information you do not want that provider to receive.

Health Connect, camera and notifications

With your permission, Setvanta writes exercise sessions and body-weight records to Health Connect. The audited app requests write access for those two data types; it does not request Health Connect read access. You can revoke access in Android settings or Health Connect. Removing a record from Setvanta may also remove a record it previously wrote to Health Connect.

Camera access supports user-initiated scanning and photo capture. Imported or captured progress media is stored locally and can be included in a user-selected export or optional server synchronization. Local reminders and rest alerts use notifications and may use an exact alarm or foreground service while timing. A paired device can also send a device identifier and rest-timer request to its chosen server for a remote alert.

Backup, export and sharing

You can export a JSON or ZIP backup through the system share sheet, optionally including media. Automatic backup may write a dated JSON copy to Documents or a folder you select. Android system backup may copy the app's training-state mirror into the backup associated with your device account; custom media files are excluded by the audited backup rules. Files or data you choose to share with another app, storage service or recipient are then handled by that recipient. Keep exports secure; they may contain sensitive fitness information.

Security, retention and deletion

The Play build requires HTTPS for server pairing and blocks cleartext traffic. Information on your device stays until you delete it through the app, remove the app or use device backup controls. Uninstalling may not erase copies already exported, backed up by the operating system, written to Health Connect, sent to an AI provider or synchronized to a server you operate. Use that server's controls to delete remote copies and contact its operator for retention questions. To remove local training data, use Settings → Data & backup → Reset everything; to stop server synchronization, use Settings → Account → Disconnect. You can revoke camera, notification and Health Connect permissions in Android settings.

Advertising and analytics

The Setvanta mobile build contains no advertising integration or configured analytics.

Contact and changes

Contact PRIMESOFT INTERNATIONAL at primesoftintel@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests. This policy may be updated when the app or its data flows change; the current version will carry its effective date.
